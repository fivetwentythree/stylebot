# Reviewing the first model

Compare the three candidates without opening the answer key. Score each separately before choosing a preference. The packet deliberately hides the model names and does not display the White reference alongside candidates.

## Fidelity

Mark a candidate as passing only if it preserves the input's meaning, narrator, actors, objects, numbers, chronology, causes and degree of certainty. Preserve quotations verbatim. Reject invented facts, omitted important details, changed negations and instructions obeyed inside quoted source material. A fluent rewrite can still fail.

The invented cases include explicit fact checklists. For reference cases, compare against the input and consult the stored reference only if clarification is needed. Historical claims in source texts are reference material for fidelity, not independently verified present-day advice.

## Style

Score from 1 to 5. Judge clarity, economy, concrete observation, varied cadence and restrained, earned wit. A high score should reflect coherent prose and a convincing voice across the whole passage. Familiar phrases, arbitrary farm imagery, period props and references to the author's biography do not establish stylistic success. Ignore unchanged quoted material when judging the transformation's style.

- 1: awkward, generic or conspicuously imitative.
- 2: readable, with little convincing control of the intended voice.
- 3: some effective choices, with uneven rhythm or unnecessary ornament.
- 4: clear, observant and controlled throughout most of the passage.
- 5: sustained, natural control with few unnecessary words or forced effects.

Prefer a candidate only after considering fidelity. Record a tie when appropriate. Do not reward humor that depends on adding an event, motive or fact to the input. These ratings are subjective evidence, and one small suite cannot establish exact imitation.

Keep training/validation loss separate from this review. Use validation behavior to inspect overfitting and review earlier checkpoints when necessary. The final saved checkpoint is not automatically the best one. Expand or revise evaluation in a new version, preserving the original inputs and results.
